Jewellery Care Tips

  • Apply perfume and cosmetics before wearing jewellery.
  • Keep away from water, sweat and chemicals.
  • Wipe with a soft dry cloth after each use.
  • Store in an airtight pouch or box with anti-tarnish paper.
  • Keep sets separately to avoid scratches.
